# Akiha 2.0 — implementation status

Updated: 2026-09-08

## Complete

- Symmetric Thu/Vinh couple membership and RLS.
- Multiple photo moments per person per day.
- One photo plus optional caption per moment.
- Idempotent upload request IDs and durable IndexedDB camera draft.
- Shared Today feed with signed images and Realtime refresh.
- Unseen count, soft read receipt, PWA app badge where supported.
- Heart, hug and laugh reactions without public counts.
- Short partner replies per photo.
- Calendar day albums with stacked 1/2/3+ thumbnails.
- Owner-only caption edit and moment deletion.
- Shared JSON, CSV and ZIP exports.
- Symmetric daily reminders and private new-photo push notifications.
- Akiha AI retained on DeepSeek V4 Flash and clearly separated from partner replies.
- Admin, rewards, streak, protected-day, garden and legacy recap routes removed.
- Production build, TypeScript, ESLint, unit and Edge Function checks passing.

## Deferred intentionally

- Native Android photo widget.
- Video uploads.
- Public sharing or friend graph.
- “Ngày này năm trước” and a redesigned shared monthly recap.
